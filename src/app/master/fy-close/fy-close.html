Financial Year Close

Finance submits a completed year; the CFO then finalizes or rejects it. A submitted year is locked — no asset, depreciation or accounting entry can be dated into it. On finalization the year's Fixed Assets Schedule is frozen. The CFO can undo a finalization within 24 hours if an amendment is needed; after that it is permanent.
